# Glossary: subscriptions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Subscription | A customer's standing agreement to receive coffee regularly until they cancel it. |
| Plan | What the subscription sends and how often: one product, a quantity, an interval. It applies to deliveries that have not happened yet. |
| Delivery | One scheduled shipment of a subscription, with its own date, contents and status. When it is prepared, it becomes an order. |
| Order | A single purchase with its own payment, stock and shipping. Not a synonym for delivery. |
| Pause | The subscription stops sending and charging until the customer resumes it. |
| Skip | One delivery is left out; the next one follows the plan. |
| Extra | A one-off product added to a single delivery, not to the plan. |
